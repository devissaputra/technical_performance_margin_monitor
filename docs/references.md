# References and Data Sources

- Saha, B., & Goebel, K. (2007). *Battery Data Set*. NASA Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center, Moffett Field, CA.
- NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence. *Prognostics Data Set Repository — Batteries*. Official archive: `https://phm-datasets.s3.amazonaws.com/NASA/5.+Battery+Data+Set.zip`.
- NASA Open Data Portal. *Li-ion Battery Aging Datasets*. The dataset documents the EOL criterion as 30% fade from 2.0 Ah rated capacity to 1.4 Ah.
- Goebel, K., Saha, B., Saxena, A., Celaya, J. R., & Christophersen, J. P. (2008). Prognostics in battery health management. *IEEE Instrumentation & Measurement Magazine, 11*(4), 33–40. DOI: 10.1109/MIM.2008.4579269.
- NASA. *Systems Engineering Handbook*, NASA/SP-2016-6105 Rev 2. NASA defines Technical Performance Measures as monitored performance measures compared with anticipated/current and future values to confirm progress and identify deficiencies.
- NPR 7123.1. *NASA Systems Engineering Processes and Requirements*. Technical Performance Measures definition and technical-management requirements.
